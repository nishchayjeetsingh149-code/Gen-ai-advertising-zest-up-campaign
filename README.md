# ZestUp Status — GenAI Advertising Campaign

## Overview

ZestUp Status is a **GenAI-assisted advertising campaign** developed for ZestUp Lemon Mint Energy Drink.

The campaign transforms the brand name **“ZestUp”** into a youthful digital status expression:

> **“Current Status: ZestUp.”**

## Campaign Objective

To create a contemporary FMCG advertising campaign that connects ZestUp with everyday moments of young Indian consumers through digital-status language and GenAI-assisted creative development.

## Target Audience

Young Indians aged 18–25, including:
- College students
- Interns
- Early-career professionals
- Young urban consumers

## Creative Concept

### The Status Comes to Life

The Hero Key Visual brings a digital status interface into the physical world, with the **ZestUp can as the main visual hero**.

## Role of GenAI

GenAI was used to support:
- Campaign ideation
- Copy exploration
- Visual concept development
- Hero KV generation
- Creative iteration
- Visual refinement

Human creative judgment guided **strategic selection, art direction, evaluation, typography, product hierarchy, and final creative decisions.**

## Key Learning

This project helped me understand how **Generative AI can support the advertising workflow while human creative judgment remains essential** for strategy, brand consistency, art direction, and final decision-making.
